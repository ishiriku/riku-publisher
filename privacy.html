Riku Publisher — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-09-29

Riku Publisher (“the App”) is a personal, command-line tool that runs only on its developer’s own computer. It uploads videos that the developer has produced to the developer’s own YouTube channels (“いしりく” and “かぞえてみた”) using the YouTube Data API. The App is not offered to other users.

1. Information the App accesses

  • OAuth 2.0 authorization for the developer’s own Google account, with the scopes youtube.upload and youtube.readonly.
  • Basic information about the authorized channel (channel ID and title), used only to confirm that a video is sent to the intended channel.
  • The processing and privacy status of videos the App uploaded, used to confirm the upload finished.

The App does not access comments, subscribers, viewers’ personal data, or any other user’s account.

2. How information is used

The information is used solely to upload the developer’s own videos, set their title, description, thumbnail and publishing schedule, and confirm the result.

3. Storage and sharing

  • OAuth tokens are stored only on the developer’s local computer, outside any synced or shared folder.
  • No data is sent to any server other than Google/YouTube APIs. No data is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.
  • Upload records (video ID, URL, title, status) are kept locally as the developer’s own work log.

4. Revoking access and deletion

Access can be revoked at any time from Google Account → Third-party connections. Deleting the local token file removes all stored authorization data.

5. YouTube and Google policies

The App uses YouTube API Services. By using the App you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. See also the Google Privacy Policy.

6. Contact

Questions about this policy: open an issue on GitHub.
